Graph-Weighted CCA

A Closed-Form Method for Inductive Attributed Graph Embedding

Jianjin Deng · Di Ming

Independent Researcher   |   Chongqing University of Technology

Problem

Inductive graph embedding must generalize to unseen nodes without learning per-node embeddings or retraining.

  • Classical methods (DeepWalk, node2vec) are transductive
  • Deep SSL methods require GPU training, augmentation, hyperparameter tuning
  • Linear methods (SGC) ignore graph structure in alignment
Research Question

Can inductive attributed graph embeddings be learned with a simpler, closed-form method while exploiting graph structure?

Method

Graph-Weighted CCA inserts graph similarity into the cross-covariance:

Tr(W⊤ X₁⊤ M X₂ H)
  • M = graph proximity (random-walk)
  • X₂ = propagated features (P^K X)
  • Closed-form: single p×p SVD
  • Deterministic, inductive by construction

Complexity: O(K|E|p + np² + p³)

Core Innovation

Replacing ordinary cross-covariance X₁⊤X₂ with graph-weighted cross-covariance X₁⊤MX₂ aligns not just same-node pairs but cross-node pairs (i,j) weighted by graph proximity—structurally similar nodes pull together across views without iterative training.

Key Contributions

  • Graph-weighted objective in cross-covariance
  • Closed-form solver via truncated SVD
  • Empirical validation on 5 benchmarks
  • Strict inductive protocol with re-run baselines
